# How to use this guide

OpenRoboto works with local operators to record first-person video of real work. Skilled workers in real businesses wear a head-mounted camera and do their jobs the way they normally would. The footage is used to train robots and embodied AI models.

The operator runs this on the ground: finding sites, signing them, managing the devices, training the workers who wear them, and uploading the data every day.

## Roles

| Role | Responsible for |
|---|---|
| OpenRoboto contact | Registers sites and workers, issues device QR codes, sets up upload accounts, shares acceptance results and handles settlement |
| Operator | Pitches and signs sites, trains workers, manages devices, uploads data every day |
| Site manager | Provides the site's details and decides who wears the device |
| Worker | Wears the device and does the job as usual |

One operator can run several sites at once. Uploading can be done by the operator or by a named upload lead.

## Terms used here

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Site | One physical business location. Each branch of a chain is its own site |
| Worker | The person wearing the device. Must be someone who actually does this job at this site |
| Scene | One kind of work setting and work content, such as auto repair, phone repair or a restaurant kitchen. Data volume and quotas are counted by scene |
| Accepted hours | Recording time that passed review and counts as usable. This is what you are paid on |
| Contact | The person OpenRoboto assigns to you. Ask them first about anything |

## Which source wins

For device operation and upload, the GI Labs documentation is the reference: [EGO1 documentation](https://docs.gilabs.xyz/docs/ego1). If this guide and the official docs disagree, follow the official docs and let your contact know so we can update this guide.

Contracts, rates and settlement cycles are set by the agreement you sign with OpenRoboto.
