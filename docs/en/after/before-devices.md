# Before the devices arrive

Get all of this ready before the devices land, so you can set them up and test-upload on day one and start recording the next day.

|---|---|---|

## Confirm upload bandwidth first {#bandwidth}

This is the most important step, and it has to be settled before the devices arrive. Without enough bandwidth, no amount of recording gets uploaded.

| Devices uploading at once | Minimum upload speed | Roughly, as broadband is advertised |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3 MB/s | about 24 Mbps |
| 5 | 15 MB/s | about 120 Mbps |
| 10 | 30 MB/s | about 240 Mbps |

- Broadband is usually sold in Mbps; 1 MB/s is about 8 Mbps
- It's the **upload** speed that matters, not download. Many home plans have fast download but only a few dozen Mbps up, so ask before signing up
- Run a speed test to measure the actual upload speed
- If a site's connection is too slow, bring the cards to one place with a good connection and upload them together

## Site details (one entry per site)

| Field | What to provide |
|---|---|
| Name | The name people actually call it |
| Address | Down to street and number. City-only addresses are sent back |
| Business | One or two sentences on what the business does and what work the staff mainly do. OpenRoboto picks the category and venue type |
| Staff count | Roughly how many regular staff: under 15, 15–100, over 100 |
| Business registration | Sites with 15 or more staff must provide the registered business name and registration number. Small informal traders and home workshops under 15 staff may not have one; say so |

Other rules:

- Register each branch of a chain separately
- Don't register the same place twice; it splits the site's data in two
- Mistakes can be corrected later, and the ID stays the same
- **Never make up a registration number.** If there isn't one, say so and your contact will handle it under the rules

## Worker details (one entry per person, all five required)

| Field | What to provide |
|---|---|
| Name | Real name |
| Job | What they actually do, such as baker, auto technician, tailor |
| Age range | Five-year bands, such as 26–30 |
| Gender | Male / female / prefer not to say |
| Handedness | Right / left / ambidextrous |

Workers must be people who actually do this job.

## Accessories

| Item | Quantity | Who covers it | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Power bank | 1 per device | Reimbursed by OpenRoboto | Keep each paired with one device. Enough capacity for a full working day |
| SD card | 2 per device | Reimbursed by OpenRoboto | One records in the device while the other is being uploaded; swap daily |
| Card reader | 1–2 per upload computer | You | For copying cards |
| Drive | Enough for one round of cards | You | One device recording a full day produces about 110 GB |
| Phone | At least 1 | You | For pairing, setting the clock and scanning QR codes |
| Computer | At least 1 | You | Mac, Linux or Windows 10/11 |

Label every device, power bank and SD card with a number so they're easy to match up and hand over.

## Agree these with the site in advance

- Which workers will wear the device and which roles will rotate
- Roughly what time recording starts and ends each day
- Where devices will be charged and stored
- Which areas are off limits: toilets, changing rooms, and anywhere the site prefers not to record
- Roughly how many weeks this round lasts, and that devices move on afterwards

## Trial new scenes first

If we haven't collected this scene before, record 2 hours with one device and wait for the review to pass before rolling out. See [Trial new scenes first](/en/before/scenes#trial).
