# Acceptance and settlement

Accepted hours come from the GI Labs review, and settlement follows that review. For the calculation, see [How you get paid](/en/before/payment).

## The review report

- GI Labs issues a review report every day, with the accepted hours for each recording
- Your contact passes the results on and tells you which scenes need to change, and which sites need more or fewer devices
- Reasons for rejected segments are in the report. Use them to fix what's wrong

## Common rejection reasons and fixes {#invalid-reasons}

| Reason | Fix |
|---|---|
| Hands often out of frame | Tilt the camera lower; remind the worker to keep hands and workpiece in front of them; turn on hand detection |
| Work too simple | Change role or scene. See [What work counts](/en/after/daily-operations#what-counts) |
| Scene already saturated | Move to another scene |
| Segments too short or fragmented | Keep recording continuously; don't switch on and off for each step |
| Inappropriate clothing in frame | Remind people on site; stop recording immediately if anyone isn't properly dressed |
| Staged or slowed-down work | Tell the worker to work exactly as usual |
| Wrong resolution | Check it's 1728×1200, and check again after a restart |
| Date shows 1970 | Connect the phone at the start of every day |

## Failed data

- Data that fails review has to be recorded again
- **Two failed reviews in a row can lead GI Labs to pause all collection**

## Settlement

- OpenRoboto settles with you based on the review reports
- Settlement cycle, reconciliation and payment method are set by your agreement
- If you dispute a review result, raise it within the time set in your agreement, with the recording ID and device serial number

## Records to keep {#records}

| Record | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Daily upload records (`gi-ingest history` or the portal's `Deliveries` page) | Reconciliation |
| Review reports | Reconciliation and fixes |
| Signed consent forms (if any) | Reference |
| Site agreements | Compliance |
| Device handover forms | Device responsibility |
| Your payment records to sites and workers | Your own reconciliation |
