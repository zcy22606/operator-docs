# Requirements, benefits and cautions

## Who this suits

Mainly two kinds of people:

- **People with access to factories and commercial sites.** They know many owners of factories, repair shops, workshops and restaurants, and can get devices on site directly
- **People with strong field sales, direct sales or telesales experience.** They can cold-call or walk in, sign many sites quickly, and keep opening new ones

You don't need a technical background. If you can use a phone app and copy files on a computer, that's enough.

## Basic requirements

| Requirement | Details |
|---|---|
| Legal entity | Able to sign contracts and receive payment as a company or registered sole trader |
| Scene pipeline | Scenes ready to start before you sign. See [how many you need](/en/before/scenes#how-many) |
| Field management | Someone who can visit sites daily to train workers, check devices and handle problems |
| Upload setup | A computer (Mac, Linux or Windows 10/11), enough disk space, and **at least 3 MB/s of upload bandwidth per device**. See [upload bandwidth](/en/after/upload#bandwidth) |
| Responsiveness | Able to act on acceptance feedback or rotation notices the same day or the next |

## What you get

- **Income based on accepted hours.** Data that passes review gets paid. See [How you get paid](/en/before/payment)
- **Devices provided by OpenRoboto.** You don't buy devices (as set out in your agreement)
- **Power banks and SD cards are reimbursed.** Card readers, drives and other accessories are your own responsibility
- **A complete process and tools.** Scene selection, device setup, upload and review all have a set method, and your contact supports you throughout
- **Referral rewards.** Bring in new operators through the [referral program](/en/business/referral)
- **Something for the sites too.** Sites and their workers get paid, which makes the first conversation easier

## Things to watch

### Quality decides income

Only accepted hours get paid. Recording more doesn't mean earning more; recording the right things does. Data that fails review has to be recorded again. **Two failed reviews in a row can pause all collection.**

### Hard lines

| Line | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Anyone in frame dressed inappropriately | Stop recording immediately. Three detected nudity incidents end the partnership |
| Staged work or people brought in to act | Data is discarded |

### Each scene and each buyer has a ceiling

Each buyer will only take so much data from any one scene. Once that's full, further recording doesn't count as accepted hours. Simpler scenes fill up faster. So you need as many scenes as possible, with devices rotating and spreading across them, rather than sitting in one or two. See [Quotas and rotation](/en/after/daily-operations#rotation).

### Look after the devices

Once devices are handed to you, you are responsible for them. Loss and damage are covered in your agreement, and you sign a [device handover form](/en/business/templates/device-handover) when you receive them.

### Expect a ramp-up

The first few weeks go into pitching sites, training and getting devices set up, so income builds gradually. Start with two or three scenes and expand once things run smoothly.
