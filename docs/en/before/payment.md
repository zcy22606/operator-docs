# How you get paid

This page explains the method only. Rates, incentive amounts and settlement cycles are set by the agreement you sign with OpenRoboto.

## The formula

```
Amount for the period = accepted hours × agreed rate + incentives (if your agreement includes them)
```

### Where accepted hours come from

- You upload everything you record, but only the parts GI Labs reviews and accepts count as **accepted hours**
- Workers record through their whole shift. Review picks out **skill segments of 30 seconds or more, built around one outcome**. Simple work in between isn't paid, and isn't penalized either
- Each buyer only takes so much data from any one scene. Anything beyond that doesn't count
- Each site has a total hours cap. See [site caps](/en/before/scenes#site-cap)
- Accepted hours come from the GI Labs review report, and OpenRoboto settles on that report

### Acceptance rate

```
Acceptance rate = accepted hours ÷ total hours uploaded
```

This is the clearest measure of how well an operator is doing. The review report gives the reason for every rejected segment. Fix those and the rate goes up.

## Incentives

If your agreement includes incentives, they are usually based on the dimensions below. Amounts are in your agreement.

| Dimension | What it measures | How it's calculated |
|---|---|---|
| Utilization | Are devices being used | Accepted hours ÷ device capacity (devices × agreed standard hours per month) |
| Quality | Is the data usable | Acceptance rate |
| Diversity | Is there enough variety | Number of active scenes in the period. A scene only counts above a minimum number of accepted hours |
| Tenure | Is the partnership steady | Months of continuous partnership; how much delivery volume swings |

Two thresholds:

- **Acceptance rate below 50%: no other incentives are paid for that period**
- **Average accepted hours below 25 per device per week: you don't qualify for incentives**

Splitting one scene into several names, bringing in extra people to pad numbers, or cramming at month-end doesn't count.

## Accessories and costs

### Reimbursed by OpenRoboto

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Power banks | 1 per device |
| SD cards | 2 per device |

How reimbursement works and its limits are set by your agreement.

### Your own costs

| Cost | Details |
|---|---|
| Site and worker pay | Fees to sites and recording pay to workers; you agree these with each site |
| Card readers, drives | For copying and storing cards |
| Staff | Time spent visiting sites, training and uploading |
| Internet and computers | Broadband and computers for uploading |
| Travel | Getting to and between sites |

### Deciding site and worker pay {#site-pay}

There are three common approaches. You can combine them:

| Approach | Good for | Watch out |
|---|---|---|
| Share of accepted hours | Getting the site to care about quality | Share review results with the site |
| Fixed pay per recording day or hour | Sites that want something simple and predictable | You carry the quality risk, so manage the floor closely |
| One fee per round | Small shops, short rounds | Agree up front how long the round runs and which roles it covers |

## How to earn more

1. **Get the acceptance rate up first.** Read the daily review and fix the reasons it gives
2. **Don't let devices sit idle.** Have the next scene ready before you need it
3. **Open more scenes.** More scenes means slower saturation
4. **Deliver steadily over time.** Operators who deliver consistently are better placed to get more devices
