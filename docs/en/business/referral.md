# Referral network and program

Devices use up the available work quickly, so you always need new sites coming in. The most reliable source of new sites is introductions from people you've already worked with. This page has two parts: building your own referral network, and the OpenRoboto referral program.

## 1. Building your own referral network

### Who can introduce you

| Source | Who they can introduce | How to ask |
|---|---|---|
| Owners of sites you've worked with | Peers, upstream and downstream businesses | Ask at the end of a round; a smooth round has the best success rate |
| Workers who've worn the device | Former colleagues, fellow apprentices' shops | Ask in normal conversation |
| Parts suppliers, wholesalers | Their customer businesses | Offer a small introduction fee, or send them customers |
| Trade associations, chambers of commerce | Groups of member businesses | Give a short talk explaining the project |
| Other operators | Sites outside your area | Swap leads; see the referral program below |

### How to run it

- **Keep a site list.** For each site, record who introduced it, the industry, staff count, how many scenes it has, and how far the conversation has got. The [referral registration form](/en/business/templates/referral-form) has a usable format
- **Give the introducer something back.** Once an introduced site is signed and recording, give the introducer a fee or a gift. You set the amount; make it clear and follow through
- **Tell the introducer when it works out.** Let them know their introduction paid off
- **Reputation matters most.** Pay on time, treat workers well, close rounds properly. That's what makes owners introduce their friends

## 2. OpenRoboto referral program {#program}

### What you can refer

| Type | What it is | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| Operator referral | A new operator (person or team) collecting in another city or area | People who know site owners elsewhere |
| Site referral | A site you can't take on yourself, passed to OpenRoboto or another operator | Sites outside your area, or when your devices are fully booked |

### One level only

Referral rewards are **one level only**. Accepted hours from the people or sites you refer count toward your reward; anything they go on to refer does not.

### When a referral counts

| Type | Condition |
|---|---|
| Operator referral | The referred operator signs with OpenRoboto and reaches ____ accepted hours within ____ days of signing |
| Site referral | The site signs, passes its trial, and reaches ____ accepted hours |

### How to refer

1. Fill in the [referral registration form](/en/business/templates/referral-form) and send it to your contact
2. Your contact checks that no one else has already registered the same operator or site
3. Your contact gets in touch with the referral, assesses it and signs it
4. Once the condition is met, the reward is paid with your settlement

### Rules

- **First registered, first counted.** If several people refer the same operator or site, the first confirmed registration counts
- **Registration expires:** if there's no signed agreement within ____ days, the registration lapses and someone else can refer them
- **No self-referral.** Setting up another entity of your own, or having relatives sign on your behalf, doesn't count
- **Existing partners don't count.** Sites already working with OpenRoboto or another operator, and operators already signed, can't be referred
- **Quality counts too.** If a referred party is paused or terminated over data quality, unpaid rewards are cancelled
- **No false referrals.** Made-up sites or false information lose referral eligibility, and paid rewards are recovered
- OpenRoboto may adjust this program with advance notice. Referrals already completed are settled under the rules in force at the time
