The parties agree as follows on first-person work recording at Party B's premises.

### 1. Scope

1. Party A will arrange for Party B's staff to wear head-mounted cameras and record first-person video during their normal work at Party B's premises (the "Recordings").
2. The Recordings are used to train and evaluate robots and AI models, and for related research and development.
3. Roles covered in this round: ____________________
4. Number of staff taking part: about ____.

### 2. Term

1. This round runs from ____ / ____ / ______ to ____ / ____ / ______.
2. During the round, devices rotate between roles and staff. Once a scene has reached a certain amount of recording, Party A may end recording for that role early or move the device to another site.
3. The parties may agree to extend at the end of the term.

### 3. Party B's obligations

1. Provide true details of the site's name, address, main business, staff count and business registration.
2. Assign staff who actually do the work in question. No substitutes and no staged work.
3. Ensure that participating staff take part voluntarily.
4. Provide a place for the devices to be charged and stored.
5. Not change device settings, and not take apart, copy or delete data from the devices.

### 4. Party A's obligations

1. Provide the recording devices and handle their setup, training and day-to-day maintenance.
2. Pay as set out in this agreement.
3. Make sure workers follow the clothing, privacy and other recording rules, and stay out of toilets, changing rooms and other private areas.
4. Use encrypted transfer and other appropriate security measures for the Recordings.
5. Avoid recording payment QR codes, phone numbers, payment details and similar information as far as possible.

### 5. Payment

1. Payment method (choose one or combine):
   - ☐ Share of accepted hours: ____ per accepted hour, based on the review results Party A provides
   - ☐ Recording pay: ____ per recording hour / ____ per recording day
   - ☐ One fee for this round: ____
2. The portion paid to participating staff: ____________________, paid ☐ by Party A directly to the staff ☐ by Party B to the staff.
3. Settlement cycle: ____________________
4. Payment method: ____________________

### 6. Data rights

1. All rights in the Recordings and related data belong to Party A. Party A may provide, license or transfer them to its partners (including OpenRoboto and its data customers) for the purposes in Clause 1.
2. The Recordings will not be published or used to evaluate, promote or advertise Party B or its staff. Use of Party B's name or logo requires Party B's separate written consent.

### 7. Confidentiality and privacy

1. Each party keeps the other's business information confidential.
2. Party A handles staff personal information in line with applicable data protection law.
3. If Party B finds anything in the Recordings that should not have been recorded, it may notify Party A, and Party A will deal with it where feasible.

### 8. Devices

1. The devices are owned by Party A or its designee. Party B holds them only for the term of this agreement.
2. Normal wear and tear is Party A's responsibility. Where devices are lost or damaged through Party B's intent or gross negligence, Party B compensates at ____ per device.
3. When the agreement ends or devices are moved, the parties sign a device handover form.

### 9. Termination

1. Either party may end this agreement with ____ days' written notice.
2. If one party seriously breaches this agreement, the other may end it immediately.
3. On termination, work already completed is paid as set out in this agreement.

### 10. Disputes

The parties will try to resolve any dispute by discussion. If that fails, the dispute goes to ____________________.

### 11. General

1. This agreement is made in two copies, one for each party, and takes effect when both parties have signed.
2. Anything not covered may be dealt with in a supplementary agreement.
