## Consent to first-person work recording

**Recording organizer (operator):** ____________________

**Site:** ____________________　**Address:** ____________________

**My name:** ____________　**Job:** ____________

### 1. What is recorded

I understand and agree that while working at the site I will wear a small head-mounted camera and work as I normally do. The camera records what is in my line of sight, mainly my hands, the tools I use and the items I am working on. It may also record sound, the surroundings and other people who appear in view.

Recording does not take place during breaks, when there is no work, or in toilets, changing rooms and other private areas.

### 2. Personal information collected

In addition to the recordings, the following is collected to categorize and count the recordings:

- Name (used only for registration and checking; not provided to others with the data)
- Job
- Age range (five-year bands)
- Gender ("prefer not to say" is an option)
- Handedness

### 3. Purpose

The recordings are used to train and evaluate robots and AI models, and for related research and product development. The recordings will not be published and will not be used to evaluate my work performance.

### 4. Sharing with third parties

I agree that the recordings and the information above (except my name) may be provided to the operator's partners, including OpenRoboto and its data customers. These partners may be located in other countries. Partners must use the data only for the purpose in this form and take appropriate security measures.

### 6. Voluntary participation and withdrawal

- Taking part is entirely voluntary. Not agreeing, or withdrawing, will not affect my job or my normal pay
- I can withdraw this consent at any time by telling the site manager or the operator. After that I will not wear the device
- Recordings already made and delivered before I withdraw may no longer be deletable. I may ask the operator to delete recordings not yet delivered, where feasible

### 7. My responsibilities

- Work as I normally would, without acting or deliberately slowing down
- Follow the clothing rules and avoid payment QR codes and payment details where possible
- Use the device with care and report any problems promptly
