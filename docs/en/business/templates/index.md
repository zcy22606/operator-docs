# Agreement templates

::: warning Have a lawyer review before use
These templates are drafting aids only, not legal advice. Rules on personal data, image rights, employment and cross-border data transfer differ by country. Have a local lawyer adapt them before anything is signed. Fill in the blanks (____) for your situation.
:::

| Template | Signed by | When |
|---|---|---|
| [Worker recording consent](/en/business/templates/operator-consent) | The worker (optional) | When needed, before the worker first wears a device |
| [Site cooperation agreement](/en/business/templates/store-agreement) | Operator and site | After the trial passes, before deployment |
| [Device handover form](/en/business/templates/device-handover) | Operator and OpenRoboto, or operator and site | When receiving, returning or moving devices |
| [Referral registration form](/en/business/templates/referral-form) | Referrer | When referring a new operator or site |

## Key points

- **The consent form is optional.** Not every worker has to sign one. Use it when the site or the worker wants something in writing; if signed, the worker signs it personally, not the owner
- **No one under 16 can be a worker.** Keep minors out of frame as far as possible
- If a worker no longer wants to wear the device, switch to someone else and tell your contact
- The pay terms in the agreement must match how you actually pay
- Photograph or scan signed documents and keep them as described in [Records to keep](/en/after/acceptance#records)
